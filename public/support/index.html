Help

Contact hemû support

For help with your account, listings, or messages, email hello@hemu.krd.

Describe what happened and include your app version, whether you use iPhone or Android, and any error message. If you send a screenshot, hide private messages, phone numbers, and other personal information.

Never send us your password, one-time sign-in code, or payment details.

Report a listing or a user

Use the report option in the app to send the listing or conversation to our moderation team. You can also block a user in the app.

Account deletion and privacy

To delete your account, follow our User Data Deletion Instructions. If you cannot access the app, the same page explains how to request deletion by email.

For information about personal data, see our Privacy Policy. The Terms of Service explain the rules for using hemû.
