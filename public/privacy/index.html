Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how hemû (“hemû,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects personal data when you use the hemû mobile application, website, and related services (the “Service”).

1. Data we collect

Account and sign-in data. Depending on how you sign in, we collect your phone number, email address, or an identifier and basic profile information supplied by a sign-in provider such as Facebook or Google. We also keep your chosen display name, profile photo, language, city, and account settings.

Marketplace content. We collect listings, photos, descriptions, prices, categories, saved items and searches, reports, and other content you submit. Before listing photos are displayed, image metadata such as embedded GPS information is removed.

Messages and interactions. We process messages and related conversation information so users can communicate. We also process actions such as listing views, favorites, reports, blocks, and notification preferences.

Location data. You may choose a city manually. If you use “current location,” the app requests foreground location permission and reads a low-accuracy location on your device to identify the nearest supported city. The Service receives the resulting city selection, not your exact GPS coordinates. You can deny location permission and select a city manually.

Technical and security data. We collect information needed to operate and protect the Service, such as IP address, app version, operating system, locale, device or installation identifiers, session and authentication records, push-notification tokens, request logs, and security or abuse signals.

Usage and diagnostics data. We may collect limited product events and diagnostics, such as screen routes, feature interactions, category and city identifiers, crash information, and a normalized search query truncated to a limited length. We do not send phone numbers, email addresses, message bodies, exact GPS coordinates, or listing free text to product analytics. Session replay is not used.

Website preferences. The website may store your language choice in local browser storage. The public landing page does not currently use advertising cookies.

2. How we use personal data

We use personal data to:

  • create and secure accounts and verify sign-ins;
  • publish, search, organize, and display marketplace listings;
  • enable messages and other interactions between users;
  • provide location-relevant results and remember your preferences;
  • send requested authentication messages, service communications, and notifications;
  • detect fraud, spam, abuse, prohibited content, and security incidents;
  • moderate content and enforce our Terms of Service;
  • measure reliability and understand how the Service is used;
  • respond to support, privacy, and legal requests; and
  • comply with applicable law and protect users, hemû, and others.

3. When we share data

We do not sell personal data. We share data only in the following circumstances:

  • With other users. Listings, public profiles, and the content you choose to publish are visible to users. Messages are shared with their intended participants. Your sign-in email address and phone number are not displayed publicly.
  • With service providers. Vendors help us provide hosting, database and media storage, image delivery, authentication, email and phone verification, push notifications, content moderation, support, analytics, diagnostics, and security. They receive only the data reasonably needed to perform their services and are expected to protect it.
  • For legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law or valid legal process, investigate fraud or harm, enforce our agreements, or protect the rights and safety of users, hemû, or others.
  • For a business change. Data may be transferred as part of a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality and legal safeguards.
  • With your direction or consent. We may share data when you ask us to or otherwise consent.

4. Facebook and other sign-in providers

If you choose Facebook Login, hemû receives the information authorized through Facebook, which may include your Facebook user identifier, name, profile picture, and email address, depending on the permissions you grant and the information available on your account. We use this data to create or connect your hemû account, authenticate you, prevent abuse, and provide the Service. We do not use Facebook Login data for advertising.

Facebook and other sign-in providers process information under their own privacy policies. You can manage the connection in the provider’s settings. To remove the Facebook-linked data held by hemû, follow our User Data Deletion Instructions.

5. Data retention

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including operating the Service, maintaining security, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations.

  • Authentication challenges and related security records are kept for limited periods, generally between 1 and 90 days depending on their purpose.
  • Inactive push-notification tokens are removed after 90 days.
  • Unattached uploads are generally removed after 24 hours.
  • When a listing is deleted, its content and original media may remain in access-restricted systems for up to 90 days while deletion and backup-cleanup processes complete.
  • When an account is deleted, a minimal deletion record is retained for up to 30 days. Messages already sent remain available to conversation participants under an anonymized user, preserving their conversation history.

Some information may be retained longer where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or enforcement. Backup copies are isolated from ordinary use and removed according to their lifecycle.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or a copy of your personal data, or to object to certain processing. You may also have the right to complain to the privacy or data-protection authority where you live.

You can update several profile and preference fields in the app, control location and notification permissions through your device, and delete your account from hemû Settings. See our User Data Deletion Instructions for the deletion process and retention details. To make another privacy request, email contact@botan.dev. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

7. Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal data, including access controls, encryption in transit, private storage for original media, short-lived authentication credentials, and monitoring for abuse. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International processing

hemû and its service providers may process data in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers. The protections and legal rules in those countries may differ from those in your country.

9. Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed to children who are not legally permitted to use it or consent to data processing in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal data from such children. If you believe a child has provided personal data contrary to this section, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Service or applicable law changes. We will publish the updated policy with a new effective date and provide additional notice when appropriate.

11. Contact us

For questions or requests about privacy, contact contact@botan.dev.
