Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AHMAD BOTAN, a French individual entrepreneur registered under SIREN 834 031 726 and operator of hemû (“hemû,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), collects, uses, shares, and protects personal data when you use the hemû mobile application, website, and related services (the “Service”). AHMAD BOTAN is the controller responsible for deciding why and how this personal data is processed.

1. Data we collect

Account and sign-in data. Depending on how you sign in, we collect your phone number, email address, or an identifier and basic profile information supplied by a sign-in provider such as Facebook or Google. We also keep your chosen display name, profile photo, language, city, and account settings.

Marketplace content. We collect listings, photos, descriptions, prices, categories, saved items and searches, reports, and other content you submit. Before listing photos are displayed, image metadata such as embedded GPS information is removed.

Messages and interactions. We process the text, listing details, private photos, and locations you choose to send in chats, together with conversation information needed to deliver and display them. Private chat photos are available to the intended conversation participants through access-controlled links. We also process actions such as listing views, favorites, reports, blocks, and notification preferences.

Location data. You may choose a city manually. If you use “current location” to select a city, the app requests foreground location permission and reads a low-accuracy location on your device to identify the nearest supported city; that city-selection flow sends the resulting city, not the device coordinates. Separately, when you choose to share a location in a chat, the Service receives and stores the latitude and longitude you send, along with any city or place name. A shared location may reveal your precise location to the other participant. You can deny location permission, select a city manually, and choose not to share a location in chat.

Technical and security data. We collect information needed to operate and protect the Service, such as IP address, app version, operating system, locale, device or installation identifiers, session and authentication records, push-notification tokens, request logs, and security or abuse signals.

Sign-in security (Cloudflare Turnstile). When a Turnstile challenge is shown during sign-in, Cloudflare processes browser and network signals, including IP address, browser information, and the site being visited, to detect automated traffic and improve its bot detection. See Cloudflare’s Turnstile Privacy Addendum for details.

Product analytics (Amplitude). When the optional Amplitude integration is enabled in a production build, we send a bounded event dictionary for app launch, onboarding, sign-in, profile saves, listing selection/contact intent, favorites, search outcomes, saved searches, posting progress, chat sends, notification opens/preferences, and known client operation failures. Amplitude associates signed-out events with a randomly generated Amplitude SDK device identifier. When you are signed in, we set the Amplitude user ID to your stable hemû account identifier so we can understand account-level activation and retention. For privacy and app-store disclosure purposes, account-identifier, product-interaction and device-ID data are used for analytics and linked to your account when signed in. Automatic event capture and session replay are not enabled.

We configure Amplitude not to receive listing, message, media, or search content; display names, phone numbers, email addresses, or other contact data; city, GPS, or other location data; advertising identifiers; or platform identifiers such as IDFV or App Set ID. The only account metadata sent to Amplitude is your stable hemû account identifier, your public username when available, and a limited flag showing whether a username exists. Events also include limited labels for the app platform, build environment, and app-language category (English, Sorani, Kurmanji, or other). Automatic collection of device manufacturer, model, operating-system version, carrier, device language, and locale is disabled. We reset the analytics identity on sign-out and account deletion. We do not use Amplitude for cross-app tracking. The currently configured Amplitude project uses its US data region.

Crash diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics). We use Firebase Crashlytics separately to understand crashes and technical failures. Crashlytics remains the destination for error diagnostics and sanitized navigation breadcrumbs; those errors and breadcrumbs are not sent to Amplitude. Crashlytics may process the technical and diagnostics data described in this policy.

Website preferences. The website may store your language choice in local browser storage. The public landing page does not currently use advertising cookies.

We use personal data to:

  • enable messages and other interactions between users;
  • provide location-relevant results and remember your preferences;
  • send requested authentication messages, service communications, and notifications;
  • detect fraud, spam, abuse, prohibited content, and security incidents;
  • moderate content and enforce our Terms of Service;
  • measure reliability and understand how the Service is used;
  • respond to support, privacy, and legal requests; and
  • comply with applicable law and protect users, hemû, and others.

3. When we share data

We do not sell personal data. We share data only in the following circumstances:

  • With other users. Listings, public profiles, and the content you choose to publish are visible to users. Messages are shared with their intended participants. Your sign-in email address and phone number are not displayed publicly.
  • When a chat is reported. Authorized human moderators can access reported chat content, including its messages, photos, and shared locations, through audited moderation tools to investigate the report and take action.
  • With service providers. Vendors help us provide hosting, database and media storage, image delivery, authentication, email and phone verification, push notifications, content moderation, support, analytics, diagnostics, and security. They receive only the data reasonably needed to perform their services and are expected to protect it.
  • In push notifications. Chat notifications can include the sender’s name and message text, or a description of a shared listing, photo, location, or offer. This notification content passes through Expo, Apple Push Notification service (APNs), and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), as applicable, for delivery. It may appear on your lock screen or other notification previews, depending on your device’s notification and preview settings.
  • For legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law or valid legal process, investigate fraud or harm, enforce our agreements, or protect the rights and safety of users, hemû, or others.
  • For a business change. Data may be transferred as part of a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality and legal safeguards.
  • With your direction or consent. We may share data when you ask us to or otherwise consent.

4. Facebook and other sign-in providers

If you choose Facebook Login, hemû receives the information authorized through Facebook, which may include your Facebook user identifier, name, profile picture, and email address, depending on the permissions you grant and the information available on your account. We use this data to create or connect your hemû account, authenticate you, prevent abuse, and provide the Service. We do not use Facebook Login data for advertising.

Facebook and other sign-in providers process information under their own privacy policies. You can manage the connection in the provider’s settings. To remove the Facebook-linked data held by hemû, follow our User Data Deletion Instructions.

5. Data retention

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including operating the Service, maintaining security, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations.

  • Chat messages are kept while both accounts exist, subject to the specific limits below and deletion or moderation actions.
  • Locations shared in chat are redacted after 90 days: their coordinates, city, and place name are cleared.
  • Chat photos are redacted after 365 days, then their full-size and thumbnail files are removed from storage.
  • After an account-deletion request, a 30-day retention grace period applies before erasure of related chat content on both sides, copied listing details, listing content and contact/location details, and associated profile data. This is a retention window, not a way to restore the account. Empty conversation or deleted-content placeholders may remain so the other participant’s account continues to work.
  • Offer and agreement records may retain anonymous terms, status, and event dates for one year from the original resolved or created event. These records exclude free text and identifiers linking them to an account, conversation, or listing.
  • Free-text report notes are erased after 30 days; report reason codes may remain. Authentication and administrator audit records have a one-year retention window, with deleted-account free-text details and IP addresses erased after the account-deletion grace period.

Content is made inaccessible or redacted before asynchronous physical deletion finishes. Deletion jobs may require more than one processing pass. Backup copies follow a separate backup lifecycle; account deletion does not mean that every external backup, another participant’s saved copy, or an operating-system notification is immediately removed. Some information may be retained longer where required by law. See our User Data Deletion Instructions for the request process.
