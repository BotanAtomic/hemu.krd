Privacy

User Data Deletion

Last updated: August 8, 2026

You can permanently delete your hemû account and associated personal data from inside the hemû app.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open hemû and sign in to your account.
  2. Open Account, then select Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and select Delete account.
  4. Confirm the warning and complete the identity check requested by the app.
  5. Confirm deletion. You will be signed out when the request is complete.

This cannot be undone. If you later return to hemû, you will need to create a new account.

If you cannot access the app

Email contact@botan.dev with the subject “Hemu data deletion request”. Include the name on the account and the email address or phone number used to sign in. Do not send your password or a verification code. We may ask you to verify that you control the account before processing the request.

What happens after deletion

  • Your profile is anonymized, and your sign-in identities, active sessions, and registered device tokens are removed.
  • Your active listings are deleted and disappear from the marketplace.
  • Messages you already sent remain visible to the people in those conversations, but they are attributed to an anonymized user. This preserves the other participant’s conversation history.
  • A minimal deletion record containing an internal user identifier and deletion dates is retained for up to 30 days, then removed.
  • Deleted listing content and original media may remain in access-restricted systems for up to 90 days while deletion and backup-cleanup processes complete.
  • We may retain limited information for longer only where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or enforcement of our agreements.

Facebook sign-in data

If you used Facebook to sign in, deleting your hemû account through either method above also removes the Facebook-linked sign-in identity held by hemû. Removing hemû from your Facebook settings alone may stop future access but does not replace a hemû account-deletion request.

Need help?

Contact contact@botan.dev. Never send us your password or one-time verification code.
