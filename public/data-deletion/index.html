Privacy

User Data Deletion

Last updated: October 23, 2026

You can permanently delete your hemû account and associated personal data from inside the hemû app.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open hemû and sign in to your account.
  2. Open Account, then select Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and select Delete account.
  4. Confirm the warning and complete the identity check requested by the app.
  5. Confirm deletion. You will be signed out when the request is complete.

This cannot be undone. If you later return to hemû, you will need to create a new account.

If you cannot access the app

Email hello@hemu.krd with the subject “Hemu data deletion request”. Include the name on the account and the email address or phone number used to sign in. Do not send your password or a verification code. We may ask you to verify that you control the account before processing the request.

What happens after deletion

  • Your profile is anonymized, your active listings disappear from the marketplace, and device/session access is revoked. Sign-in identity removal and media deletion are processed through our deletion jobs.
  • After a 30-day retention grace period, we erase related chat content on both sides of your conversations, including messages, private photos, and shared locations. We also erase copied listing details, listing content and contact/location details, associated profile data, and report notes by or about your account. The grace period does not let you recover the account.
  • Empty conversations, deleted-content placeholders, and anonymized internal profile records may remain to keep the other participant’s account working. They do not preserve the erased message text, photos, or locations.
  • Anonymous offer and agreement terms, status, and event dates may remain for one year from the original resolved or created event, without free text or identifiers linking them to an account, conversation, or listing.
  • Authentication and administrator audit records have a one-year retention window. Deleted-account free-text details and IP addresses are erased after the account-deletion grace period.
  • Content is made inaccessible or redacted before asynchronous physical deletion finishes. Processing may take more than one pass. Backup copies follow their separate lifecycle; this does not immediately delete another participant’s saved copies or notifications already delivered to a device.
  • Some information may be retained longer where required by law. Our Privacy Policy also explains the normal 90-day shared-location, 365-day chat-photo, and 30-day report-note limits.

Facebook sign-in data

If you used Facebook to sign in, deleting your hemû account through either method above also removes the Facebook-linked sign-in identity held by hemû. Removing hemû from your Facebook settings alone may stop future access but does not replace a hemû account-deletion request.

Need help?

Contact hello@hemu.krd. Never send us your password or one-time verification code.
